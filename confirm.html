Wake Up

You’re in. And that took courage.

Signing up for something like this is not a small thing. In a world that profits from your distraction, choosing to go deeper is an act of quiet rebellion. I do not take that lightly and I am grateful you are here.

Over the coming weeks I am going to walk you through Wake Up one song at a time. The music, the research behind it, and the rabbit holes worth going down. Check your inbox for your first email.
